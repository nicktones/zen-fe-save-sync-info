Windows PC and Android handheld

Your Eden saves, on both devices.

Zen-FE Save Sync connects the Windows Zen-FE frontend and Android Zen-FE Companion using your Google Drive account. It synchronises Nintendo Switch in-game saves used by the Eden emulator.

Sign in on both devices, select the Eden user save folder once, and link the handheld. All existing non-empty game saves in that folder are discovered together; new saves are discovered on later syncs.

Preserve your progress

Save revisions are encrypted before upload. Offline changes are queued for retry. Different progress made on both devices is kept as a conflict, with history and local backups available for recovery. Sync waits while Eden is running.

Your Google account

The feature requests only drive.appdata: access to its own private application storage in Google Drive. It does not request access to your ordinary Drive documents. You can disconnect from the apps or revoke access in your Google account.

Read the privacy policy for what is stored, how it is protected, and how to remove cloud data.
