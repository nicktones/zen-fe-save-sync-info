Save-sync privacy policy

Effective 4 October 2026. This policy describes the Google Drive save-sync feature of Zen-FE and Zen-FE Companion, maintained by the Zen-FE project.

What the feature accesses

With your permission, the apps read and write the Eden save folders you select. They use Google’s https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata permission to read and create files in this application’s private Drive storage. They do not request access to your ordinary Drive files, contacts, mail or calendar.

What is uploaded and why

The apps upload encrypted snapshots of selected game-save directories to synchronise progress between your devices. The encrypted manifest includes game/title and linked-player identifiers, save-file contents and hashes, revision ancestry, a device label and timestamps. The Drive file name contains a format identifier, a recovery-key-derived vault identifier and a random revision identifier. Google can see file metadata such as names, sizes and upload times.

ROMs, emulator firmware and keys, shaders, caches and emulator configuration are excluded from this save-sync feature. Your Google password is entered only into Google’s sign-in interface.

Local storage and protection

Snapshots and manifests are encrypted using AES-256-GCM before upload. The recovery code is stored locally using Windows DPAPI or Android Keystore protection. Windows OAuth tokens and client settings are protected by DPAPI; Android uses Google’s authorization service. Local save directories, backup copies, queues, history and diagnostic status remain on your devices. Device security and secure recovery-code storage remain important.

Sharing and permissions

Cloud saves are sent directly from your devices to Google Drive. There is no Zen-FE-hosted save-storage server. This feature does not sell save data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Its use of Google user data is limited to providing save sync and recovery.

When you choose Send setup to handheld, the apps send the recovery code, game labels and linked-player information over the existing paired TLS connection. Google tokens are not sent to the other device. Android Usage access is used locally to guard against modifying saves while Eden is active; it is not uploaded by this feature. Google handles data on its services under its own privacy policy.

Retention and removal

Uploaded revisions are retained for history and conflict recovery; this feature does not automatically prune them. Disconnecting or revoking access stops cloud access but does not itself delete saved revisions or local backups.

To remove cloud application data, open Google Drive on the web, choose Settings → Manage apps, locate this application, and use its Options menu to delete hidden app data where available. You can revoke the app’s access through your Google account’s third-party connections. Deleting cloud data affects cloud history for devices using that account; local saves and backups remain separately on your devices.

Local queues and backup data can be removed from the device’s app-data storage after syncing or exporting anything you want to retain. Uninstalling an app or deleting its data can remove locally stored recovery information. Keep a recovery-code backup before doing so.

Questions and updates

Contact nicktones@gmail.com for privacy questions. Do not send passwords, tokens, recovery codes or save files unless you specifically decide to share them. This page will be updated if the save-sync data practices change.
